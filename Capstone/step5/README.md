## Raw sources

We began by gathering three diverse corpora of image–caption pairs to fuel our cross-modal retrieval experiments:

- **Stable Diffusion Prompts**: a CSV of synthetic prompts generated by the Stable Diffusion model, downloaded from Kaggle ([https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/stable-diffusion-image-to-prompts/data](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/stable-diffusion-image-to-prompts/data)) as `custom_prompts_df.csv`.
- **MS COCO 2017 Captions**: the official COCO caption annotations, available at the MS COCO website ([https://cocodataset.org/#download](https://cocodataset.org/#download)) in JSON format (`captions_train2017.json` and `captions_val2017.json`).
- **Flickr-30k Captions**: a pipe‐delimited CSV from Kaggle ([https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/hsankesara/flickr-image-dataset/data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/hsankesara/flickr-image-dataset/data)), containing 158,915 human‐written captions in `results.csv`.

## Cleaning

Once loaded, we cleaned each dataset through a consistent pipeline:

1. **Missing values**: we dropped any entries lacking either an `image_id` or a `caption`.
2. **Length filtering**: based on our inspection of token‐length histograms, we enforced maximum lengths of 100 tokens for SD prompts, and 50 tokens for COCO and Flickr-30k captions, removing outliers beyond these thresholds.
3. **Normalization**: all text was lowercased and trimmed of leading or trailing whitespace to ensure uniform formatting.
4. **Deduplication**: we removed exact duplicates of `(image_id, caption)` pairs to avoid redundant examples.

After cleaning, the resulting tables for each corpus were saved as Parquet files on the Desktop:

- `sd_clean.parquet` (100,000 rows)
- `coco_clean.parquet` (591,555 rows)
- `f30k_clean.parquet` (158,784 rows)

---

## Merged Dataset

The three cleaned Parquet tables were then loaded, annotated with a new `source` column (indicating SD, COCO, or F30k), and concatenated into a single unified dataset of 850,339 rows. To ensure compatibility with downstream tools, the `image_id` column was cast to string type. Finally, we exported the full merged dataset to the Desktop as `crossmodal_dataset.parquet`.
